# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and technical leads evaluating Alex for Full Stack roles in Spain and remote positions across the EU. They usually arrive with little time, need to understand what Alex builds, and want evidence they can inspect before opening the CV or starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Alex Micó Robles as a Full Stack developer who turns concrete problems into usable web products with React, Node.js and Python. Success means a visitor can identify his role, inspect four representative projects, understand his contribution and reach the correct CV or contact route without internal company operations becoming his public identity.

Anuubis Solutions remains visible as Alex's current employer in the experience timeline and on his GitHub profile. Fleet metrics, client operations, internal tooling and the former WordPress case study are not part of the public proposition.

## Brand Personality

Sober, approachable and product-led. The voice is direct and specific, with calm confidence rather than campaign language. The interface should feel personal enough to belong to one developer and rigorous enough for a technical reviewer to trust.

## Anti-references

- The current operations-dashboard framing: fleet counters, server metrics, monitoring signals and repeated terminal metaphors.
- Generic developer portfolios built from neon gradients, badge walls, fake consoles or interchangeable stack slogans.
- Agency campaign pages whose art direction is louder than the work being shown.
- Internal Anuubis tools, client operations or closed operational metrics presented as Alex's personal projects.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work before describing the stack: real product screens and public source are the primary evidence.
2. Let four projects carry the narrative with an explicit hierarchy: Strev and Sereno are primary cases of equal weight; SaveMyMoneyNow and Atalaya provide supporting evidence.
3. Put employment in context: Anuubis belongs in experience, not in the hero or project selection.
4. Make the first useful screen count: role, value, selected work and a clear next action must appear early.
5. Keep every claim inspectable: prefer public repositories, live products, case studies and stable qualitative results over private counters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, 44px touch targets, readable contrast in both themes and 200% zoom. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; language and theme changes must remain understandable without animation. Do not encode meaning through color alone.
